# Release preparation: 24 October 2026

Dilemma Dynamics now documents both completed temperature studies: **3,552 episodes**,
with separate 24-block / 1,776-episode datasets at temperatures 1 and 0. The
[combined report](../public/research/paper/report.pdf), [data README](../public/research/README.md),
[methods](../public/research/protocol.md), app Results/Replay, judge walkthrough
and submission draft include the temperature sensitivity results and limitations.
Original temperature-one numeric data remain byte-preserved; temperature-zero
files and matched comparisons are additive. Research execution source, runtime,
preparation, claims, clocks and raw evidence remain unchanged. No inference was launched.
