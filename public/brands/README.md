# Technology logos

These small logos identify the services and models used by Dilemma Dynamics.
They are locally hosted so displaying the app does not request third-party images.
The marks remain the property of their respective owners and are not covered by
the project's MIT license.

- Nebius: official [website logo](https://nebius.com/logo.svg), retrieved 24 October 2026.
- NVIDIA: horizontal `n24-nvidia-logo` SVG symbol from the official
  [NVIDIA website](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/about-nvidia/legal-info/logo-brand-usage/),
  retrieved 24 October 2026. The symbol is wrapped as a standalone SVG; its artwork,
  proportions and colors are retained.
